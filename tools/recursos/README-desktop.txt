HEADSETBRIDGE FOR XBOX - Which shortcut to use
==============================================

1. PLAY - Xbox audio through your PC headset
   For gaming. Use your Xbox controller and play on your TV while the audio
   comes through your PC headset. Double-click to start. Double-click again to stop.

2. MUSIC - Xbox Spotify through your PC headset
   Listen to Spotify (or any other music app) running on your Xbox through
   your PC headset without putting the console into Remote Play mode.
   If Spotify isn't already playing, it launches automatically.
   You may notice a brief half-second glitch at each handoff (roughly once a minute).
   Double-click to start. Double-click again to stop.

3. Greenlight - see your Xbox on your PC
   The full streaming client, with video, for when you want to see your Xbox
   screen on your PC. You don't need it for the other two shortcuts.
   Greenlight MUST BE CLOSED when you use 1 or 2.

The first time you use 1 or 2, if you have never signed in, your browser opens
microsoft.com/link and a small window shows a code: enter it, sign in with your
Xbox account and the audio starts. You only need to do this once.

Use only one at a time. The first two are the same program (2.4 MB, no window),
installed in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\XboxSoloAudioRust.
If something goes wrong, the log is at %LOCALAPPDATA%\XboxSoloAudio\rust.log
